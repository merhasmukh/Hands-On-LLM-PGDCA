# Task 6: AI Ethics & Limitations Debate Prep

**Objective:** Critically analyze the challenges and risks of deploying Large Language Models in the real world.

**Instructions for Students:**
1. Research and read a recent news article about an AI failure or controversy (e.g., a chatbot giving bad legal advice, bias in a hiring algorithm, deepfakes in media, or data privacy leaks).

**Deliverable:** 
Write a short essay (1-2 pages) covering the following points:
- Summarize the news story.
- Identify which specific limitation from Unit 1 this represents (Hallucination, Bias, Privacy Concerns, or Explainability).
- Propose one rule, guideline, or technical guardrail that developers could implement to prevent this issue from happening again in the future.
