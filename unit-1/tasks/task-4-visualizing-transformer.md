# Task 4: Visualizing the Transformer (Conceptual Diagramming)

**Objective:** Reinforce your understanding of the Transformer architecture and the Attention mechanism.

**Instructions for Students:**
1. Without looking directly at the course notes, try to draw a high-level flowchart of a Transformer architecture on a piece of paper or using a diagramming tool. Be sure to include the main path: Input → Embedding → Positional Encoding → Encoder → Decoder → Output.
2. Write a sentence of your choice that contains a word with multiple meanings (e.g., "The bank of the river was muddy, so I couldn't sit on the bank.")

**Deliverable:** 
1. Submit your diagram (a photo or a digital export).
2. Write a short paragraph explaining how the **Self-Attention** mechanism helps the model understand the correct meaning of the ambiguous word in your sentence based on its surrounding context.
